    About this map

    At the end of the year 3600 a dead mountain woke. The Jylefjön range had not known fire for three hundred million years; the ash that rose from it did not settle.

    Seven years later the coast lives in an endless dusk. Where a Tirsång burns, a fire-sun built at ruinous cost, there is still harvest. Everywhere else the villages empty out.
